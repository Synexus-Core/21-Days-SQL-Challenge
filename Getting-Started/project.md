# 🏗️ The Capstone: Synexus Core Operations Database

Over the next 21 days, you are not writing random queries for disconnected tables. Every day, you will be constructing the backend data infrastructure for the **Synexus Core Operations Platform**—a centralized management system built to handle technical community members, events, projects, and attendance tracking.

## 📊 The Project Evolution

**Phase 1: The Core Foundation (Days 1-11)**
You will build the raw schema. You will design the tables for `members`, `events`, `projects`, and `attendance`. You will establish the Primary and Foreign Keys that link a specific member's QR attendance scan to a specific community event, and you will learn to retrieve and filter that data efficiently.

**Phase 2: Advanced Execution & Optimization (Days 12-21)**
As the Synexus community scales, simple queries are no longer enough. You will write complex subqueries to identify the most active project contributors, use Window Functions to rank event turnout, optimize slow member searches with Indexing, and finally, assemble it all into a massive query.

## 🎯 The Grand Finale (Day 21)
On the final day, you will act as the Lead Data Engineer. You will write a single, highly optimized, multi-table Business Intelligence (BI) report query. 

This report will pull together data from active members, their roles, their project contributions, and their event attendance to generate a real-time analytics dashboard summary for the core committee. 

By completing this, you will possess a complete, enterprise-grade database schema and the advanced SQL scripts required to manage an organization's operations—a perfect addition to your professional portfolio.
